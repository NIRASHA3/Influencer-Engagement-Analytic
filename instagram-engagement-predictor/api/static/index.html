Prediction Workflow

The scoring engine follows a transparent pipeline so you can trace why confidence shifts and how the final value is produced.

1. Feature intake and sanitation Checks required fields, removes invalid numeric values, and standardizes category tokens.
2. Distribution-aware preprocessing Applies trained transformations so live inputs match the feature scale used during training.
3. Dual-model inference pass Runs XGBoost and Random Forest in parallel to produce independent engagement estimates.
4. Agreement and spread analysis Measures distance between models and classifies the result as tight, moderate, or divergent.
5. Feature influence explanation Ranks the strongest positive and negative drivers to support result interpretation.

Operational confidence bands

High confidence typically reflects aligned model outputs and in-range features. Lower confidence indicates model spread or feature drift and should trigger manual review.

  • Output packet: predicted rate, agreement score, and quality status
  • Warnings are shown when values exceed practical training ranges
  • Use A vs B compare mode to test scenario sensitivity quickly

Decision Playbook

Translate model output into fast campaign decisions using three clear gates: stability, risk, and unit economics.

1. Stability gate Review model agreement gap. Lower spread indicates more stable forecasts and stronger decision confidence.
2. Risk gate Use the risk badge from the Decision Panel. High risk should trigger manual review before budget allocation.
3. Economics gate Compare CPE and Efficiency Index in the Scorecard. Prioritize scenarios with lower CPE and stronger efficiency.
4. Optimization gate Use Goal-Seeking suggestions to adjust creative inputs and rerun Scenario A vs B before final sign-off.
GO Low risk and acceptable CPE. Approve influencer for promotion.
PILOT Medium risk or moderate CPE. Run a small-budget trial first.
HOLD High risk or weak economics. Request revisions or shortlist alternatives.

Practical campaign rule

Professional shortlist quality improves when candidates pass all three gates across more than one scenario.

  • Shortlist only candidates passing risk and cost thresholds
  • Use Scenario A vs B for brief-level sensitivity checks
  • Keep training drivers visible before final campaign approval
